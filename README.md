### About

I build the hardware of robots that have to work in competition.

I study Robotics Engineering at the Federal University of Rio Grande (FURG), in southern Brazil, and I am part of [FBOT@Work](https://github.com/FBOTWork), the team's industrial category, which competes in RoboCup@Work. I joined the hardware team in March 2025 and became vice-captain of the category in July 2026.

- 🔧 Power and connection boards for the robot, designed in EasyEDA, with STM32, Arduino and step-down converters
- 🧩 Mechanical design in Onshape, from single parts to a complete robot, 3D printed and machined
- 🤖 Python, C and ROS to integrate the hardware with the rest of the robot
- 🧭 Vice-captain of a 22-person subteam: meetings, schedule and technical decisions
- 🌐 Built the website of FURG's Memory Center (NUME) with Joomla and JavaScript
- 💬 Portuguese (native) · English (advanced) · Spanish (intermediate)

### Achievements

- 🥇 **Brazilian Robotics Competition 2025** — RoboCup@Work (Entry Level), Vitória
- 5th place — **RoboCup 2025**, RoboCup@Work, Salvador
- Team result: 4th place — **RoboCup 2026**, Incheon
